Madison County is the starkest process story in WNC — worse than Asheville's.

## What happened

Sheriff Buddy Harwood's office deployed **15 Flock cameras** countywide (in use by March 2026) **with no commission vote and no public discussion** — announced via the sheriff's Facebook page. When dozens of residents came to the June 9, 2026 commissioners meeting to object, **Chairman Michael Garrison refused to let them speak individually — "You will not speak on Flock tonight"** — allowing one seven-minute spokesperson, and arguing the board merely funds the Sheriff and has *no oversight* of his technology purchases. The story went national via [404 Media](https://www.404media.co/you-will-not-speak-on-flock-tonight-county-commissioner-refuses-to-let-residents-opposing-flock-speak-at-meeting/).

The numbers, from published audit logs: **1,200+ nationwide-database searches in 60 days; 3,302 searches across 33 offense categories in five months — in a county of about 20,000 people.** ([Madison for Privacy](https://www.madisonforprivacy.org/) · [DeFlockILM tracker](https://deflockilm.org/flock-in-north-carolina/))

And the records blackout: the sheriff's office has **refused the bulk of public records requests** — contract, expenditures, audits, policies — which opponents say violates NC public records law. The contract's dollar value is unknown because the county won't release it.

## Who's fighting

**[Madison for Privacy](https://www.madisonforprivacy.org/)** (madisonforprivacy@proton.me) — records requests, public-comment organizing (March 10 and June 9, 2026 meetings), Fourth Amendment framing, coordination with Mountain Indivisible. During the national week of action: "Popsicles for Privacy," organized by shepherd Mary Gibson. ([Atlanta Press Collective](https://atlpresscollective.com/2026/08/15/flock-week-of-action/))

## The leverage

1. **Sheriff Harwood is on the November 2026 ballot** (vs. Robin Lyles, R) — the deployment-without-process and the records refusals are campaign questions nobody has put to either candidate on record.
2. **The budget is the vote the commission never took**: the cameras ride the Sheriff's general appropriation, renewed each June. Garrison's "we have no oversight" is false in exactly one place — the dollars.
3. The commission meets the **2nd Tuesday, 6:45 PM public comment** ([video: Madison County Government on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@madisoncountygovernment3990) — the March 10 Flock comments are on tape). Commissioners: boc@madisoncountync.gov.

## Watch items

- Whether the county's paper of record (News-Record & Sentinel) ever covers the fight — it hasn't yet, a notable silence.
- Marshall, Mars Hill, and Hot Springs each have mapped Flock cameras (likely the county's); none of the three town boards publishes meeting video at all.
