**Candidate for City Council, Nov 2026** · sitting Buncombe County Commissioner (District 2) · tied for the lead in the March primary (~16%)

## Surveillance record

As a county commissioner, Ball sits on the body that funds the Sheriff's ~1,800-camera Fusus network — the system Asheville's RTIC extends. **No vote or public statement from him on Fusus, Flock, or the RTIC has been found** (as of Aug 17, 2026).

## In his words

His [Asheville DSA questionnaire response](https://avldsa.org/2026-city-council-primary-questionnaire-responses/) addressed ICE — families deserving "safety, not terror" — but said nothing about surveillance technology. His [Chamber survey](https://www.ashevillechamber.org/news-events/2026-city-council-candidate-survey-drew-ball/) is likewise silent on it.

## The question for him

He's the one council candidate with an actual record *adjacent* to this issue — county budgets that sustain the Fusus network. Has he ever questioned it? Would he vote for a surveillance-oversight ordinance in Asheville ([[Promise Tracker]])? See [[Election 2026]] for the questions to ask.
