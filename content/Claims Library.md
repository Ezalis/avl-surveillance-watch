## The process

- The final Axon contract was **not available for council or public review** before the May 12, 2026 vote. ([GAP](https://gapavl.org/asheville-should-reject-the-proposed-rtic-axon-surveillance-expansion-until-real-oversight-and-accountability-exist/))
- **21 signed-up speakers were sent home unheard** on May 12; the item was scheduled at the end of a budget meeting and voted near midnight. ([BPR](https://www.bpr.org/politics-government/2026-05-13/asheville-council-accepts-police-tech-funding-hears-budget-proposal))
- RTIC funding first passed via **consent agenda** — the no-debate section — in March 2025. ([BPR](https://www.bpr.org/bpr-news/2025-03-26/last-night-at-council-asheville-approves-funding-for-police-surveillance-hub))

## The scale

- APD's 11 Flock cameras scanned **~3.1 million plates in 21 months** in a city of ~95,000. ([Asheville Watchdog](https://avlwatchdog.org/opinion-youre-on-camera-whether-you-like-it-or-not-but-asheville-needs-to-be-more-transparent-about-video-surveillance-proposal/))
- Asheville's plate data is shared with agencies across a **500-mile, multi-state radius**. ([Annie Dance](https://anniedance.substack.com/p/asheville-expands-regional-surveillance))

## The track record (national)

- The **LAPD walked away from Flock** in July 2026 after its Inspector General found a **32.3% false-positive rate** on stolen-vehicle alerts. ([LAPD IG audit coverage](https://knock-la.com/lapd-suspends-contract-with-flock-safety/))
- A Texas sheriff's office searched **83,000 cameras nationwide** for a woman with the logged reason "had an abortion." ([EFF](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2025/10/flock-safety-and-texas-sheriff-claimed-license-plate-search-was-missing-person-it))
- Local police nationwide ran **4,000+ Flock lookups for federal immigration purposes** — ICE has no contract; it gets access as informal favors. ([404 Media](https://www.404media.co/ice-taps-into-nationwide-ai-enabled-camera-network-data-shows/))
- Nearly **50 officers have been criminally charged** for using plate readers to track partners, exes, and family — including two NC officers charged in the last month. ([AP](https://www.spokesman.com/stories/2026/aug/16/flock-safety-tightens-safeguards-as-states-cities-/))
- **50–100+ communities** have canceled, rejected, or paused Flock contracts — roughly two dozen in 2026 alone. ([DeFlock tracker](https://deflocktheusa.com/cancellations/), [State of Surveillance](https://stateofsurveillance.org/news/flock-safety-cancel-wave-30-cities-alpr-surveillance-contracts-2026/))
