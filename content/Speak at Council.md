## The rules (learned the hard way)

Public comment is capped at **one hour per item, three minutes per speaker**. On May 12, that cap sent 21 signed-up residents home unheard. If turnout is large, arrive early, sign up immediately, and coordinate so speakers cover *different* points instead of repeating one.

## What's still unanswered

Kim Roney submitted **29 written questions** to APD in March 2025 about data access, control, and constitutional implications. They were never publicly answered. The final Axon contract was not available before the vote. The DOJ grant conditions were published only after acceptance. Asking a specific unanswered question on the record is more powerful than a general objection — it creates a paper trail.
